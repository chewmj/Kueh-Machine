Ginger & Scallion Fish 姜葱鱼

The dish she makes when someone in the family is unwell. Always steamed, never fried, "so it stays gentle on the stomach."

"一块姜, 差不多这么大" (a piece of ginger, about this big) → ~15g ginger, julienned
"两块钱的葱" ($2 worth of scallion) → ~4 stalks scallion

Steps and photos to come as I document this properly with her.
